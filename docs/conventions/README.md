# Conventions

Conventions are policy: label strings, state machines, naming patterns, and the numbers
that bound the loop. They implement the ontology's invariants for real trackers and
repositories; when they conflict with an invariant, the invariant wins and the
convention must be fixed.

- [Issue lifecycle](issue-lifecycle.md) — triage and workflow states, labels, claim
  semantics, who may move what.
- [Branching and PRs](branching-and-prs.md) — branch, worktree, and pull-request shape.
- [Review and escalation](review-and-escalation.md) — the review loop, its caps, and
  what happens when work cannot proceed.
